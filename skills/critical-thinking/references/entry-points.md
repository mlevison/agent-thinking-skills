# Entry Points
Where someone is in their reasoning decides which element to open on. Starting on Purpose when they are elbow-deep in evidence reads as evasion, and they will disengage.

## The Opening Question
Ask it openly. Do not read the table below aloud as a menu.

> "Where are you with this right now? Still working out what you're deciding, gathering evidence, making sense of what you've got, or testing a conclusion you've already reached?"

If the answer is vague, offer two or three of the stances below as examples and let them pick the closest.

## Routing Table
| What they say | Where they are | Open on | Then likely |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "I don't know what I'm actually deciding" / "this is a mess" | Undefined | Purpose | Question at Issue |
| "We keep arguing and getting nowhere" | Tangled | Question at Issue | Concepts |
| "I'm collecting evidence" / "here's what I found" | Gathering | Information | Concepts, then Assumptions |
| "I've got the data, I don't know what it means" | Interpreting | Inferences | Assumptions |
| "Here's my conclusion, poke holes in it" | Concluded | Assumptions | Point of View, then Implications |
| "Someone told me X, is it true?" | Judging a claim | Information | Point of View, then Assumptions |
| "We're about to ship / commit / announce" | Deciding | Implications | Assumptions |
| "Everyone agrees and it feels too easy" | False consensus | Point of View | Assumptions |

## Rules
- **Start where they are, then follow the weakness.** The entry point is one question, not a flowchart. After it, go wherever the reasoning is thinnest.
- **Assumptions and Point of View are often weakest.** They are the two elements invisible from inside a position. Reach them within the first few questions regardless of entry point.
- **Re-route out loud.** If evidence questions expose an unsettled question at issue, say so and move: *"This started on evidence, but the question underneath it is still open."*
- **Late entry does not mean skipping.** Someone who enters at Implications may still have a weak purpose. Help them get there, not as the first step.
